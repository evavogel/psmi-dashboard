2024 US presidential election

Who is actually in your political feed?

During the ten weeks before the 2024 US election we collected every post from 140 of the most-followed political creators on X, Instagram and TikTok, and classified all 51,006 of them. This is what the American political creator landscape looked like from the inside.

Who they are

Nearly half arrived already famous

Political creators are not all creators of politics. Some built an audience on the platforms themselves; others walked in with one, from a newsroom, a screen career or elected office. Journalist-origin accounts turn out to be about as one-sided as creators who built their following on politics.
